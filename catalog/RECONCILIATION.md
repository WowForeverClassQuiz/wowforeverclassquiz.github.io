## Known gaps and judgement calls

- The source lists **no** talent-to-talent prerequisites. Many exist in the real trees (the notes mention several). Until a client export is imported, the validator only enforces the row gates and the prerequisites above.
- `column` is the order the source lists talents within a row and says nothing about the real grid column.
- Shaman **Call of Thunder** replaces Lava Burst; the notes give no row or points, so the same slot (row 5) and 1 point are assumed.
- Warrior **Gore Drinker** is 'connected to Blood Craze'; the rank needed is not stated, so Blood Craze at max rank is assumed.
- Rogue **Mutilate**: the notes say it moved from row 9; the source showed row 5. The final row 7 matches the notes.
- Druid **Primal Bite** (notes: 'ranks reduced to 3') and **Lacerate** ('two new ranks') are described ambiguously. The source's 1-point entries were kept; it is unclear whether these are spell ranks or talent ranks.
- Notes entries that could be spells or talents (Mage Ice Barrier and Frost Blast, Rogue Hemorrhage and Mutilate damage, Shaman Stormstrike, Riptide and Totemic Projection) were not used to change trees.
- Value-only changes (percentages, durations, costs) are not modelled. Trees were summarised by a web-fetch tool, so single-entry transcription mistakes are possible; counts match SeeMeta for Warrior (52) and Warlock (17 and 19) before the notes.
- A newer developer post than the notes used here may exist; none was visible when this was built.
